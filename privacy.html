Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Personal Health Hub is a private personal-health analytics project. It is not a commercial service and is not offered to the public.

Data collected

When authorized by the user, Personal Health Hub may access health and activity information from connected services, including Oura data such as sleep, readiness, activity, heart rate, HRV, workouts, stress, SpO₂, and related account information needed for authentication.

How data are used

Data are used only for the user's personal longitudinal health analysis, visualization, and N-of-1 experiments. Data are not sold, rented, or used for advertising.

Storage

Health data are intended to be stored in the user's private notebook environment, local files, and/or private cloud storage such as the user's Google Drive. This public GitHub Pages site does not store health data.

Sharing

Personal Health Hub does not intentionally disclose health data to third parties except where necessary to use services explicitly chosen by the user or where required by law.

Retention and deletion

The user controls retention. Data can be deleted by removing the Personal Health Hub database/files from the user's storage. Access to Oura can be revoked through the user's Oura account or by removing the application's authorization.

Security

OAuth client secrets, access tokens, refresh tokens, and health databases should never be committed to this public repository.

Contact

Questions may be directed to the contact email registered with the associated Oura developer application.
